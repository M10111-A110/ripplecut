## Modeled rules (RIPPLECUT-MODELED)

* `checkoutservice = cartservice AND productcatalogservice AND currencyservice AND shippingservice AND paymentservice   (soft, non-propagating: emailservice)` — x_checkout = x_cart AND x_catalog AND x_currency AND x_shipping AND x_payment. emailservice is SOFT because PlaceOrder only logs a warning when the confirmation email fails.
* `frontend = productcatalogservice AND currencyservice AND cartservice   (soft, non-propagating: adservice, recommendationservice, shippingservice, checkoutservice)` — x_frontend models the storefront browse path (home page). Calls whose failure only breaks a specific page (cart view, checkout) or is logged and ignored are SOFT for this definition; the purchase path itself is represented by checkoutservice.
* `recommendationservice = productcatalogservice` — ListRecommendations calls ListProducts without exception handling.
* `loadgenerator = 1 (no hard inputs)   (soft, non-propagating: frontend)` — Traffic source only; its availability is not modeled as depending on frontend.

State meaning used by the model: frontend: x=1 iff the storefront browse path (home page: currencies, product list, cart) can render. ## Cartservice and Incompleteness of the Action Universe

In the canonical Online Boutique topology, `cartservice` is designated as a critical service ($C_{min}=7$). However, the modeled action universe contains **no action** that restores or bypasses `cartservice` (e.g., no in-memory fallback, no local cart cache, no bypass mechanism).

Consequently, when `cartservice` fails:

1. Every candidate action subset $B \subseteq \mathcal{A}$ leaves `cartservice` DOWN.
2. The cascade fixed point $\Phi(T_B(x(0)))$ satisfies $C(B) < C_{min}$, failing the critical preservation requirement.
3. RippleCut's exact solvers and validator prove that **no feasible plan exists** (`NO_FEASIBLE_PLAN`), certified by the monotonic upper bound certificate $\bar{C} < C_{min}$.

This is an intentional and certified architectural property of RippleCut: RippleCut strictly respects the finite action universe and never hallucinates or fabricates non-existent recovery mechanisms ('magic buttons') when an incident is uncontainable under the available operational controls.
